SECURITY RESEARCH AND ENGINEERING

How far can digital evidence actually take you?

A valid record can tell you what happened inside its scope. The harder question is what that record allows you to conclude.

The record can be true. The conclusion can still be false.

ABOUT THE LAB

Independent by design.

Ghalvera is an independent security research and engineering lab based in Madrid. We build controlled experiments to understand what digital evidence can establish, and publish the evidence and limitations with every result.

Research is conducted only in systems we own or are explicitly authorized to test.
