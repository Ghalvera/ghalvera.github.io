02 / CLAIM STATES

Different evidence.
Different authority.

Public presentation should never imply that every entry carries equal evidentiary weight.

ACCEPTED

Finding

A bounded result survived the method and review applied to it. Its stated limits remain part of the finding.

NO_VERDICT

Open study

The study exists and its protocol may be mature, but the named conclusion has not earned a verdict.

INVALID

No result

Validity, ground truth, protocol or scope failed. An invalid experiment does not become negative evidence.

NOT DETERMINED

Boundary

Questions outside the tested configuration stay unresolved instead of being silently generalized.

03 / DIMENSIONS

Ghalvera separates authorization, attribution, completeness and consequence so a vague claim of “weak evidence” cannot hide the actual inferential gap.

THE PUBLIC SURFACE IS NOT THE SOURCE OF TRUTH

Every conclusion keeps
its provenance and limits.

The publication surface summarizes work authored and reviewed in the laboratory repositories. It does not retroactively upgrade older work or imply authorization for prospective studies.
