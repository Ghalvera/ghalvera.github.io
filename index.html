02 / RESEARCH OBJECT

Verification is
not the end.

A signature can be genuine. A log can be intact. A trace can be complete under its own rules. None of those facts automatically proves authorization, attribution, completeness or consequence.

Ghalvera names the inferential step and puts it under adversarial pressure in environments we own or are explicitly authorized to test. The exploit is not the finding. The finding is what the evidence does—or cannot—justify.

07 / SOFTWARE

An instrument serves a question. A product owes maintenance to users. Neither becomes a finding by existing.
