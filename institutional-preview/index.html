Mission / Vision

Know exactly how far the evidence reaches.

We run controlled security experiments, compare system records with independent ground truth, and publish only the conclusions those records can support.

Vision

Security systems that are not only verifiable, but honest about what their evidence can establish.

Lab principles

Evidence first.

We work in environments we own or are explicitly authorized to test, preserve independent ground truth, and keep the original limits attached to every result.

The exploit is not the finding. The finding is what the evidence can justify.
