Independent security research

The record can be true.
The conclusion can still be false.

Ghalvera studies the limits of digital evidence: what a record proves, what it leaves open, and where a confident conclusion goes too far.

Mission

Know exactly how far the evidence reaches.

We run controlled security experiments, compare system records with independent ground truth, and publish only the conclusions the evidence can actually support.

Vision

Security systems that are not only verifiable, but honest about what their evidence can establish.

Research programmes

Four places where certainty often breaks.

Each programme asks one narrow question. The point is not to produce a dramatic answer. It is to find the boundary precisely.

01

Authorization

Did the relevant authority actually intend or permit the action?

identity ≠ intent
02

Attribution

Which actor or delegated identity caused the action?

name ≠ actor
03

Retrieval & completeness

Was the evidence captured — and can it actually be found when needed?

healthy ≠ complete
04

Consequence

What changed, persisted, escaped containment, or survived reversal?

stopped ≠ reversed

Method

See. Attribute. Retrieve.

First observe what happened. Then separate the actor from the identity in the record. Finally, retrieve the evidence independently. Only after that do we ask what conclusion it supports.

The lab

Experimental rigor over theatre.

Ghalvera is an independent security research lab. We work in environments we own or are explicitly authorized to test, preserve ground truth, and keep the original limits attached to every result.

The exploit is not the finding. The finding is what the evidence does — or cannot — justify.
