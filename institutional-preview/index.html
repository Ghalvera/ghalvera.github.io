Mission / Vision
Mission

Find the exact point where evidence stops supporting the claim.

We design controlled security experiments, compare the record with independent ground truth, and publish the narrowest conclusion the evidence can actually carry.

Vision

Security systems that are honest about what they know.

Verification should tell us more than whether a record is genuine. It should also tell us what that record does — and does not — establish.
